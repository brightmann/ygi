Clicking on a line intersection will place black and white stones. Stones of different colors are arranged in checkerboard order, so you can't really play.

I haven't yet managed to make black and white stones appear in turn; more thinking is needed.

I tried doing it with a selector like <code>:checked:nth-of-type(odd)</code>, but it counts all matching elements including every other one, even though it only highlights those that are <code>:checked</code>.

I couldn't reproduce the game's behavior, so it's a fail.
