I'm deliberately not posting the code with explanations and hints, so as not to spoil the joy of discovery for those who want to repeat it without peeking at the sources, which, by the way, can easily be obtained from the examples.

In my opinion, a very beautiful effect, although it has some limitations.

P.S.:
1. It eats up the computer's resources quite a bit.
2. It looks best in Webkit-based browsers; big questions about Firefox.

<b>Upd. from 5.07.2020:</b> similar rays can easily be made in SVG using a shape with a very thick <a href="/svg-decoration">dashed stroke</a> or with <a href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/conic-gradient">conic gradients</a>, but the latter currently have insufficient browser support.
