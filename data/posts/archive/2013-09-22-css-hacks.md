When the need arose to separate styles for IE8 with selectors, I found out that it ignores selectors containing <code>:root</code> and <code>:not</code>, because they're from CSS3.<!--more-->

Test:

<iframe class="live-snippet" style="height: 250px" src="../assets/demo/css-hacks/demo_1.html?css"></iframe>

It's also important to remember that if a selector consists of several classes listed comma-separated, IE8 will ignore the whole list.

The selector <code>:root .crimson, .crimson</code> will work everywhere except IE8:
